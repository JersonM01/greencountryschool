Why we teach French

French as a foreign language, to an official standard

At Green Country School, French is taught as a foreign language (FLE). Our main value is a recognised level of French, measured by the official DELF and DALF diplomas.

French as a foreign language

Pupils learn to understand, speak, read and write French step by step, with methods made for learners whose first language is not French.

One scale of levels

Progress is described with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), from A1 to C2.

Official diplomas

DELF and DALF are the official diplomas of the French Ministry of National Education for non-native speakers of French, recognised worldwide. DELF Prim is designed for primary-school pupils.

Official diplomas in French as a foreign language, from A1 to C2
